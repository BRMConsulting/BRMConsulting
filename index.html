How we work

Structured problem solving, step by step

We follow the hypothesis-driven method made famous by the world's leading strategy consultancies: define the problem precisely, break it into parts that do not overlap, test the most likely causes with data, and act on the answer. Then we add what large firms often leave out: implementation on the ground and proof of results.

  1. 1

    Define the problem

    Write one sharp, measurable problem statement with the decision-maker: what, how much, by when.

  2. 2

    Break it down

    Build an issue tree that splits the problem into parts that are mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive (MECE).

  3. 3

    Prioritise

    Form hypotheses and focus on the 20% of causes likely to explain 80% of the problem.

  4. 4

    Analyse with data

    Test each hypothesis with your records, site observation, statistics and simulation.

  5. 5

    Synthesise

    Answer first: a clear recommendation supported by three key arguments, each with cost and payback.

  6. 6

    Implement and measure

    Pilot with your team, train, standardise, and verify results against the agreed indicators at 30 and 90 days.
